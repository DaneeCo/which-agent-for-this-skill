# Agent map

As of Nov 24, 2026, from hands-on testing of each agent in real life (shopping, travel, family logistics, client work). Prices and access change monthly.

## Black Friday
- Watch a price and text me when it drops: Instinct
- Buy at an exact time (drops, trials, a virtual line): Instinct
- Add up returns and what I'm owed: Instinct
- Amazon links and search: dot on your own Mac. Amazon blocks many cloud agents (Muse since Sept 2026, Grok orders reportedly since Oct 2026). A screen agent on your own logged-in Mac has worked in testing, but nothing confirms it keeps working. Say that.

## With a partner or family
- One shared agent in a group text: Instinct
- Ask someone else's agent (agent to agent): Instinct. Consent is still a pilot, so tell your agent what it may share.

## Everyday
- Dig through texts and email: dot
- Catch what I'd miss (trash night, schedule changes): dot
- Roll up noisy group chats, WhatsApp, Slack: Grokbot
- Grab sign-in codes, send at volume: Grokbot

## Work and social
- Client work in my voice with my skills: Claude with Duet
- Tidy Instagram saves: Muse

## Watch out for
- Instinct: can't read your texts. Invite-only beta, free for now.
- dot: stalls when the Mac sleeps or locks, and approvals pile up overnight. Needs ChatGPT Pro (about $100 a month).
- Grokbot: wants the most access, and its browser logins expire (WhatsApp needs QR re-scans). X paid plans from about $30 a month. Nickname in testing: "the cowboy".
- Claude with Duet: waitlist preview for Pro or Max plans. You can't watch its browser.
- Muse: can't read WhatsApp. Free, and AI training is on by default.

## Rules of thumb
- Connector first, screen agent when there's no connector.
- Two agents on one job: one list, one sender, and check who texted last.
- Ask for one rollup twice a day, not a ping per update.
- Anything that spends money or messages people as you: set it to ask first.
