# Worked answers

## Typical
Ask: "I want to know when the stand mixer I like drops below $250 this week."

> Job: watch one price and tell you when it drops.
> **Use:** Instinct. Runner-up: dot, if you're on a Mac with ChatGPT Pro.
> **Why:** price watching and texting you back is what Instinct did most reliably in testing.
> **Say this:** "Watch this mixer and text me if it goes under $250 before Monday." (paste the link)
> **Watch out:** it alerts, it doesn't buy, unless you tell it to. If you want it to buy, set it to ask first.
> As of Nov 24, 2026.

## Edge: the map doesn't cover it
Ask: "Can an agent file my expense reports in our company's finance tool?"

> Job: enter expenses into a work finance app.
> This one isn't on my tested map. My reasoning, not a test result: look for a connector for that finance tool first. If there isn't one, Claude with Duet can drive a browser, but it's a preview and you can't watch it.
> Run one report by hand with it before you trust it.
> As of Nov 24, 2026.

## Risk flag
Ask: "Have Grok text my whole soccer group that practice moved."

> Job: message a group as you.
> **Use:** Grokbot. It sends at volume.
> **Why:** it handled bulk sends in testing.
> **Say this:** "Draft a text to the soccer group saying practice moved to 5, and show me before you send."
> **Watch out:** it messages people as you and wants big access. Set it to ask first.
> As of Nov 24, 2026.
